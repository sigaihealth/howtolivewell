A NOTE ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Useful advice should be open to everyone.

This guide is original, U.S.-focused writing inspired by the idea of making everyday advice easier to use. Every action links to a public source. It is general information, so check the linked source and ask a qualified professional about your own medical, legal, or financial situation.

Programs and rules can change and may differ by state. The guide is free, bilingual, and open source. Saved steps are kept only in your browser.
