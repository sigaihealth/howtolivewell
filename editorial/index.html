ABOUT THIS GUIDE

How we make and maintain this guide

Live Well, Here is a free English and Spanish guide for everyday life in the United States. It offers manageable starting steps, with a source beside each one so readers can check the detail for themselves.

Publisher and purpose

This is a SIG.AI project published at livewell.sig.ai. Its source code and change history are public on GitHub. The guide is meant to help someone choose a useful next step, then reach a local service, official source, or qualified professional when needed.

How the content is made

The writing is original to this project and tailored to U.S. services and everyday situations. AI tools assisted with drafting the English and Spanish copy and building the website. The actions and linked sources were checked during publication, but this guide does not claim independent review by a licensed clinician, attorney, or financial adviser.

The How to Live Better reader inspired the idea of an easy-to-browse practical guide. Its text was not copied into this U.S. version.

How sources are chosen

Each action points to a public page that supports its first step. We prefer direct pages from U.S. government agencies and other relevant public-service organizations, rather than a generic homepage. When a suitable Spanish page is available, the Spanish guide links to it; otherwise the link is marked as an English source.

A source can contain important exceptions or newer details. Programs, eligibility, deadlines, and rules vary by state, locality, and personal circumstances. Follow the linked source and the relevant local agency before making a consequential decision.

What this guide can and cannot do

These pages offer general information. They are not personal medical, legal, or financial advice, a promise of program eligibility, or an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger in the U.S., call 911. For a personal question, ask a qualified professional or the program that administers the service.

Updates and corrections

Content last updated October 2, 2026. Linked sources were checked in October 2026. Those dates describe work done on this guide; they do not guarantee that every outside page or program is still current.

To suggest a correction, open a public GitHub issue with the page link and a supporting source. Do not include private medical, financial, or identifying details in a public issue.
